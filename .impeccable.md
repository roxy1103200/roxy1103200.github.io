## Design Context

### Users
Roxy Blog readers and visitors arriving from the author's GitHub profile. Keep article content readable and the author's links easy to find.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, personal, and editorial. Keep the existing Chinese and English voice and avoid promotional framing.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the supplied blog index prototype: a dark navy canvas, cyan accents, compact navigation, rounded article cards, and a two-column desktop layout with a profile sidebar. Stack the layout on small screens. Use a local initial avatar and inline SVG icons rather than external assets.

### Design Principles
- Keep only content display, article reading, and real personal links.
- Do not add search, filtering, theme switching, comments, ads, analytics, or offline caching.
- Keep layouts responsive, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Point GitHub references to `https://github.com/roxy1103200` and keep profile links in `_config.yml`.
- Derive article and tag counts from real content; never invent project statistics.
- Preserve existing articles and their URLs. Do not restore removed article content.
- Retain the Hux Blog source credit in the footer.
