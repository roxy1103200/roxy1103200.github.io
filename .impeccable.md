## Design Context

### Users
Roxy Blog readers and visitors arriving from the author's GitHub profile. Keep the site easy to read and make the author's profile easy to find.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, personal, and editorial. Keep the existing bilingual voice and avoid promotional framing.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the dark editorial theme with a near-black canvas, readable light text, and restrained teal accents. Do not use photographic header backgrounds, About or Archive pages, or the old top-right λHUX link. Keep the navigation compact, with Home and Search. Link GitHub profile references to `https://github.com/roxy1103200`.

### Design Principles
- Keep article content as the focus when new posts are added.
- Preserve the current dark color palette, accessible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Keep layouts responsive and bilingual labels concise.
- Keep the old-site badge out of the navigation; retain the existing theme credit in the footer.
- Do not restore removed article content.
